# Privacy Policy for Herbivore

Last updated: 20 October 2026

**Herbivore does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.** There is no
account, no analytics, no tracking and no server.

## What Data We Collect

None is collected. To work, Herbivore keeps a few things on your own device (see below), and
reads product names and the shop's own dietary symbols on the shop page you're viewing, on
your device, to decide what to blur or hide. Nothing it reads is stored, except products you
flag yourself.

## How Data Is Stored

Only in your own browser, in Chrome's local extension storage. It never leaves your device and
is not synced to any account. What's stored:

- your diet mode (Vegetarian or Strictly Vegan) and display choices (Blur or Hide, minimised
  widget)
- products you flagged, products you chose to show, and keywords you chose to block
- the extra websites you asked Herbivore to "always run" on

## How Data Is Used

Only to filter shop pages the way you chose: your mode decides what is filtered, and your
flags and keywords fine-tune it.

## Third-Party Services

Herbivore does not use any third-party services. The optional "Buy me a coffee" link opens
ko-fi.com only if you click it; Herbivore sends nothing to Ko-fi.

## Data Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, because none leaves your device.

## Site Access

- Built-in UK supermarket sites, listed in the extension's details in Chrome.
- Any other site only when you click the toolbar icon (that one tab), or when you choose
  **Always run on this site** and approve Chrome's prompt for that one site. You can remove a
  site from the Help & memory page, or in `chrome://extensions` → Herbivore → Details → Site
  access.

Herbivore's widget and saved items are hidden from the shop's own scripts. The shop page can
still see the buttons and blurring Herbivore adds to its product tiles (for example a "Not
Vegan" button), so a shop could in principle infer which mode you're using.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your settings and lists stay until you remove them. You can:
- remove individual items, or reset a mode's whole list, on the Help & memory page
- export a backup file to your computer (it is never uploaded) and import it later
- uninstall Herbivore, which deletes everything it stored

## Changes to This Policy

If Herbivore's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated with a new date above,
and the change will be described in the extension's release notes before it ships.
