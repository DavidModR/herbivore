How to use it

  • Works automatically on Sainsbury's, Asda, Tesco, Ocado, Morrisons, Waitrose, Aldi and Amazon UK groceries.
  • On any other shop, click the 🌱 toolbar icon. Pin it from the 🧩 puzzle-piece menu so it's always there.
  • Something slipped through? Hover over it and click 🚫 Not Veggie / 🚫 Not Vegan. You can also block a keyword from its name.
  • Something hidden that's fine? Click 👁️ Show. A ❓ warning that's fine? Click ✓ Fine.
  • Household items (tissues, cleaning, toiletries, pet food) are skipped: Herbivore is a food filter.

A helper, not a guarantee

Herbivore reads product names and the shop's own dietary icons, so it can miss things (gelatine in sweets, animal rennet in cheese) or filter things that are fine. Always check the ingredients, especially if you have an allergy. Your settings and lists stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere.
